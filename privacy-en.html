Synth Privacy Policy

Effective: October 8, 2026

This is a translation. If it differs from the Korean version, the Korean version governs.

This document explains what information JIPGERIA (the "operator"), which makes and distributes Synth (the "app"), receives, why, and when it is deleted.

At a glance

  • If you only listen to music and watch the wallpaper, you leave nothing on the server. There is no account.
  • The titles and artists of the songs you listen to never leave your Mac. The only exception is the song information attached to a video you share.
  • Usage statistics are asked about on first launch and are sent only if you agree. You can turn them off at any time in Settings (⌘,).
  • Names and emails are received only with reports and reinstatement requests, and with feedback if you chose to include an email.

2. Information used only on your Mac

The app reads the following but does not send it anywhere.

  • The title, artist, album and position of the song now playing in Spotify or the Music app (to pick the video for that song. macOS asks for permission once)
  • The address of the frontmost tab when you press "Bring in browser tab" (to fetch the video or photo on that page. Your Mac connects to that site directly, so the site sees your IP address)
  • An address or picture you paste (⌘V)
  • The videos and photos you set, and your settings (~/Library/Application Support/Synth/)

The tools used to fetch web videos (yt-dlp and, if needed, ffmpeg) are downloaded from their distributors (GitHub and others) the first time they are needed.

3. Who processes it for us

The operator does not run its own servers and uses those of the companies below.

The data is stored in Seoul, but both companies are United States corporations. The information in section 1 (everything that reaches the server) may therefore be accessed from the United States in order to operate the servers and fix outages, and files are delivered through servers in a country near the recipient. The transfer happens when the information reaches the server (over an encrypted connection), and the two companies keep it until the operator deletes it or the contract ends. If you do not want this, turn off usage statistics and do not share videos — then nothing is transferred other than connection records.

We do not give information to any other third party, except where the law requires it, such as a court order.

5. What you can do

  • Turn off usage statistics: Settings (⌘,) → "Share anonymous usage stats". Nothing is sent from that point on.
  • Stop sharing: select the video in the app window and turn sharing off. It is deleted from the server.
  • Ask to access, correct, delete or stop processing: email the address below. Usage statistics can only be found by the installation number (a random value stored in the app's settings), so we need that number to identify yours. Report records are found by the email address you used.
  • Erase everything: stop sharing everything, then delete the app and the ~/Library/Application Support/Synth/ folder. Nothing remains on your Mac.

Requests are handled within 10 days of receipt. If you are not satisfied with how the operator handled a request, you can seek help from Korea's Personal Information Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, 118 within Korea) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (kopico.go.kr, 1833-6972).

6. Children

We do not knowingly receive information from children under 14. If we learn that we have, we delete it.

7. How we protect it

  • The app talks to the server only over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • The server's tables are set up so that the app can insert but not read. Only the operator can read usage statistics and reports.
  • The operator's administrative key is not in the app; it exists only on the operator's devices.
